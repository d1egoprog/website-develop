Hi, I'm Diego Rincon-Yanez

Researcher & Computer Scientist

I am a computer science researcher and engineer combining academic expertise with hands-on industry experience. Over more than fifteen years, I have worked as a university professor, researcher, IT architect, CTO, and full-stack engineer for multiple organizations.

I currently work as a researcher at the Institute for Data, Process and Knowledge Management at the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU Vienna). My research focuses on Neuro-Symbolic AI, bridging symbolic representation and neural learning through Semantic Web frameworks, Knowledge Graphs, vector-based representations, and reproducible machine learning processes.

I also have a soft spot for other areas of computer science, including distributed computing and high-performance computing.
