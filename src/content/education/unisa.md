# Overview

The Research Doctorate in Informatics and Information Engineering was awarded by the University of Salerno in November 2023.

The doctoral research focused on **Knowledge Graph Construction Methods over Neuro-Symbolic Artificial Intelligence**, combining symbolic knowledge representation with neural learning approaches.

## Research Focus

The doctoral research addressed:

- Knowledge Graph Construction
- Semantic Web technologies
- Neuro-Symbolic Artificial Intelligence
- Knowledge Representation
- Artificial Intelligence

## Institution

The degree was completed at the Department of Information and Electrical Engineering and Applied Mathematics, University of Salerno, Italy.

This doctoral programme belongs to the field of Information Engineering and Computer Science research.
